The room

One skeleton, one skin

Clothes, hair, and attachments are their own meshes. The bind pose is the Second Life dressing T: Z is up, the face looks down +X. Joint names follow the public base skeleton, and a worn piece bends with the same bones.

She wears a talk AO the way a Second Life avatar wears an animation overrider. The system stand is the stiff hang. The AO softens the elbow. While she speaks, a talk clip moves the free arm. A held prop is applied after that stack, so the cup still owns the hand.

  • Mira

    Orange hair, a red blouse, a black skirt. The coffee cup is the first thing she wears.

  • The witch

    Green hair, a white robe, a black belt. The hat, the wand, and the book are attachments.

  • The tiefling

    Red skin, copper hair, horns, pointy ears, and a tail. sway() wags it.

The rest of the company stands behind them. Each doll has a name, a voice, and a script. A name in the page is a dot in the script: mira, witch, tiefling, singer.

The voice

She speaks. She sings.

Preview speaks the page in the voice of a reference clip, with Zonos3-Mini. You hear the words of the page. Generate uses the speech model from Settings. Chatterbox Turbo is the start.

A line can be sung. @ names the singer. & names the song. The types are ballad, shanty, waltz, march, and dirge. The words from the mark to [end] are one song.

[&shanty @Singer]
Haul the night along.

The next verse stays in the song.

[end]

singer.guitar hangs on her chest and strums that beat. The band is a General MIDI score. The vocal is that doll's voice, locked to the tune. SoulX-Singer is used when DOLL_SOULX points at it. A singer with no clip uses Mira's voice.

The marks, the events, and the cup are written out on Doll Script.

The stage

Three views

  • Script

    The markdown. Headings, emphasis, and the square brackets are colored. The picture stays off this page.

  • Chapters

    One card per paragraph, and a small picture beside the cards. A blank line starts the next paragraph. A heading names the ones under it.

  • Scene

    The picture, full size. Click a doll. Drag on empty stage turns the camera. The wheel on her changes her size.

[@Mira] selects the mouth. [!sip] runs a function. A fence of three backticks is shown on the terminal for that chapter. One mouth moves, the one whose line is under the clock. The other doll listens.

Run

On this machine

./scripts/build.sh
./scripts/run.sh

The build fetches glslangValidator into .tools/ when it is missing, and it links the same Qt tree as MiraTalks. ./scripts/run.sh --shot /tmp/doll.png --time 3.3 writes one frame. That time is the middle of a sip when the paragraph is Mira's.
